Privacy Policy

Spin Top Arena Publisher · Last updated: November 13, 2026

1. Summary

The app only connects to the TikTok account of its creator. It does not collect, store or share any information about other people: not viewers, not followers, not commenters.

2. What the app accesses

  • user.info.basic: the creator's own basic profile, to confirm which account is connected.
  • video.upload: to send a rendered video to the creator's TikTok inbox as a draft.
  • video.list: the creator's own public videos and their counters (views, likes, comments, shares), to see which videos work best.

3. What is stored, and where

The OAuth tokens of the creator's account and the counters of the creator's own videos are stored in a private file on the creator's own server. Nothing is sold, shared or sent to any third party. There are no ads, no analytics trackers and no cookies on this site.

4. Retention and deletion

Data is kept only while the app is in use. Revoking the app's access in TikTok's settings stops all access immediately; the creator then deletes the stored tokens.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children and does not process any data about them.

6. Contact

Questions can be sent through the creator's GitHub profile: github.com/Manomania.
